# Demo guide (about 3 minutes)

1. **Problem (20s):** MLP is hard to recycle, often missed at MRFs, and has no clear destination.
2. **Dashboard (20s):** pick an MRF; explain the 4-step flow: Identify → Bale → Route → Report.
3. **Identify (50s):** click *Start simulated belt*. Say clearly the detections are **scripted**; the real version needs a model trained on MRF images. Then use *Manual check* to show the no-hardware option for pickers.
4. **Bales (30s):** enter a weight, choose a grade, click *Log bale* – shows traceability.
5. **Pathways (40s):** change a rate or share; net value updates. Stress that rates are placeholders. Set shares to not add to 100% to show the warning.
6. **Report (20s):** show the ULB report and Print.
7. **Close (20s):** ask for one pilot MRF to collect images and real offtake quotes.

**Expect these questions:** Is the AI real? (No – simulated; planned.) Where are test results? (None yet; pilot plan lists metrics.) Who buys MLP? (Pathways are known routes; buyers need validating.)
